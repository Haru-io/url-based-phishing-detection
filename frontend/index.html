PHISHING SAFETY

Stay safer before you click.

Sentinel helps you assess suspicious URLs, but a few simple habits can reduce phishing risk even further.

01

Check the URL carefully

Look for misspelled domains, unusual subdomains, extra characters, or suspicious redirects before entering sensitive information.

02

Verify unexpected messages

If a link arrives through email, SMS, or social media unexpectedly, verify the sender and destination independently before opening it.

03

Never share credentials blindly

Do not enter passwords, OTPs, or other sensitive information on a page unless you are confident the website and request are legitimate.
